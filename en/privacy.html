Privacy Policy — LUDO DZ

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Offline play

Playing against the computer or with friends on the same device works offline and sends no data anywhere.

Online play

If you choose to play online, the game creates an anonymous account automatically. No email address, phone number or password is needed. To run online matches we process:

  • A random anonymous user ID
  • The player name you type in
  • Match data: room code, dice rolls, moves and the time you were last connected to the room
  • Technical logs, such as your IP address, kept by our hosting provider

We use this data only to run online matches (matchmaking, game rules, reconnection and rematches). We do not sell it, share it for advertising, or use it for analytics or tracking. The game contains no ads, no analytics tools and no crash reporting.

Where data is stored

Online data is stored with our service provider Supabase (project hosted in the EU, Paris region). Other players in your room can see your player name.

Retention and deletion

We keep match data for 30 days after the last activity, then it is deleted automatically. Inactive anonymous accounts are deleted after 180 days. You can ask us to delete your online data by emailing midou4tv@gmail.com. Uninstalling the game removes the data stored on your device.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to send. We use it only to reply to your enquiry and handle the issue you reported. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if the game's privacy practices change, and adjust the last-updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions: midou4tv@gmail.com
