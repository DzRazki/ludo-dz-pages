Delete Account — LUDO DZ

This page lets you request deletion of your anonymous LUDO DZ account and the data linked to it.

How to request deletion

  1. Enter your player ID in the field below (or email midou4tv@gmail.com).
  2. Press “Send deletion request”.
  3. We verify the request, then delete the data.

Data that is deleted

  • Anonymous account and user ID.
  • Player name.
  • Match and game data linked to the account.

Data retention

  • Match data is deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Inactive accounts are deleted after 180 days.
  • Temporary technical logs may remain with the hosting provider for a short period, for security purposes only.

Questions: midou4tv@gmail.com
